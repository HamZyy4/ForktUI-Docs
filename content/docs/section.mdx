---
title: Section
description: Group related controls and content.
icon: Rows3
---

```luau
local Section = Tab:Section({
    Title = "General settings",
})

Section:Button({
    Title = "Apply",
    Callback = function()
        print("Applied")
    end,
})
```

Use sections to separate unrelated groups in a longer tab. Avoid adding a section around every individual control.
